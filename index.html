DNG are delighted to present No. 62 Beresford House, an exceptional two-bedroom penthouse apartment ideally positioned within the highly sought-after Custom House Square development in the heart of Dublin's IFSC.

Presented in immaculate, turn-key condition throughout, this stylish apartment has been finished to an incredible standard by the current owners and offers bright, contemporary accommodation complemented by a superb south-west facing balcony which extends the full length of the apartment.

One of the outstanding features of No. 62 is undoubtedly its extensive private balcony. Enjoying an enviable south-westerly orientation, this wonderful outdoor space benefits from recently laid artificial grass and can be accessed independently from both bedrooms as well as the open-plan kitchen/living/dining area. It provides a natural extension of the internal accommodation and, with its south-westerly aspect, is well positioned to enjoy the morning, afternoon and evening sun.

Internally, the apartment has a clean and contemporary finish throughout, with premium laminate flooring creating a seamless flow between the principal living spaces and bedrooms.

The heart of the apartment is the bright and spacious open-plan kitchen/living/dining room. The fully fitted contemporary kitchen has been cleverly designed with sleek handleless cabinetry and soft-close drawers, complemented by a tiled splashback and a comprehensive range of appliances. These include a hob, built-in extractor fan, fridge/freezer, slimline dishwasher and Whirlpool washer/dryer.

The living area is generously proportioned and features a bespoke media wall together with an attractive gas fireplace framed by distinctive Connemara stone, creating an impressive focal point within the room. Extensive glazing and direct access to the south-west facing balcony ensure an abundance of natural light throughout the day.

Both bedrooms are generously proportioned double rooms and benefit from floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes, providing excellent storage while maintaining the apartment's clean, uncluttered aesthetic. Each bedroom also enjoys its own direct access to the full-length balcony.

The accommodation is completed by a beautifully presented contemporary bathroom comprising a bath with shower above and glazed shower screen, wash-hand basin with vanity storage and WC, all complemented by stylish neutral tiling. The bathroom also benefits from having a built in speaker system which can be connected via bluetooth.

The welcoming entrance hallway provides access to the principal rooms and continues the bright, understated finish found throughout the apartment.

For additional comfort and future flexibility, the apartment is also wired for air conditioning and beneifts from one underground secure car parking space.

No. 62 offers a rare combination of penthouse positioning, immaculate presentation, excellent natural light and substantial private outdoor space in one of Dublin city centre's most convenient locations.

This is a truly turn-key apartment that must be seen to be fully appreciated. The photographs simply do not do No. 62 justice and early viewing is highly recommended.
